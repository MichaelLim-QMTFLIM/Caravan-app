About Us

Caravan was founded in 2026 by an aspiring entrepreneur named Iyad Michael Ghazal. After recognizing a gap in the local spice market, he established the company and partnered up with four of his business associates with a goal to introduce a wider-variety of spices to Philippine consumers. While Caravan offers staple off-the-shelf spices and seasonings such as pepper, salt, and chilies, the company also specializes in sourcing rare and premium spices and bringing it here into the Philippine Market. Caravan aims to provide home cooks, food enthusiasts, and professional chefs with easy access to high-quality spices that are not commonly available in local stores. Caravan also provides product bundles consisting of herbs and spices representing a country, dish, or culture.

Mission

The Caravan purpose is to address the challenge faced by cooking enthusiasts in sourcing fresh and exotic products from major retailers. We aim to deliver unique herbs, spices, and culinary ingredients through trusted suppliers, creating product bundles that represent various countries, dishes, or cultures. Lastly, we aim to provide detailed information about each herb or bundle's history and offers multiple payment methods, including online payments or cash, along with receipts.

Vision

Over the next 3-5 years, we have a vision of becoming a global business. Our customer reach will expand through international shipping, multi-currency payments, diversify our catalog with variety of products, launch an app dedicated for mobile use (iOS/Android), lastly, we aim to have partnerships with international suppliers and third-party logistics providers will strengthen our product range, affordability, and fulfillment of our said mission.
